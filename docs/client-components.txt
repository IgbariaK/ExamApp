ExamApp (Root)
 │
 ├── NavigationMenu (Global UI - adjusts based on user role)
 │
 ├── AppRouter (Handles page navigation)

 │    ├── AuthPages

 │    │
 │    └── StudentPages (Requires Student Auth State)
 │         ├── StudentDashboard (List of pending/completed exams)
 │         ├── ActiveExam (The test-taking interface)
 │         └── GradeReport (View personal results)
